# Unglued continuously shaped bamboo strips

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `31212` to `pcr.other-transportable-goods-except-metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.products-of-wood-cork-straw-and-plaiting-materials.wood-continuously-shaped-along-any-of-its-edges-or-faces-including-strips-and-friezes-f-b33b549b`. The supported product is a discrete unassembled strip from one bamboo culm with a deliberate continuous longitudinal profile. The foreground receives dry unglued blanks and covers machining, sanding, dust collection, compatible site-proven solvent-free UV polyurethane finishing when applicable, inspection and packing. Glued or engineered bamboo, scrimber, plywood, plain planing, assembled flooring, onsite drying and combustion are excluded. Upstream preparation, treatment and drying must be separately evidenced. This acceptance does not establish the whole classification leaf as methodologically covered.
