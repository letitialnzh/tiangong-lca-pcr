# CPC 3.0 03112 mapping decision

## Source leaf and proposed target

- Official CPC 3.0 leaf: “Roundwood of coniferous wood, pulpwood and wood for wood-based panels”; it includes coniferous roundwood intended for pulp, particleboard, OSB, or fibreboard. Source: https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Structure/Detail/EN/2100/03112.
- Canonical PCR: `pcr.agriculture-forestry-and-fishery-products.forestry-and-logging-products.roundwood-of-coniferous-wood-pulpwood-and-wood-for-wood-based-panels`.
- Accepted relation: `broader` (the PCR is broader than CPC 03112; corrected on 2026-10-10).

## Semantic boundary to review

- Included: coniferous industrial roundwood supplied for pulp or wood-based panels, in round/split form or, where cut directly from roundwood in the forest, chip form, all reported as solid volume under bark. Source: FAO Joint Forest Sector Questionnaire definitions, https://www.fao.org/forestry-fao/7800-0944787ecbf6036088182f841ab15fe42.pdf.
- Excluded: sawlogs/veneer logs, non-coniferous wood, wood fuel, poles/other industrial roundwood, mill-made chips/residues, and the downstream manufacture of pulp and panels.
- Route: declared forest stand and management, felling/bucking, extraction to roadside landing, product grading and scaling, with optional direct-in-forest chipping. Gate: graded material at forest roadside ready for loading to external transport; road haulage to the mill is outside this PCR. The FAO harvesting glossary defines landing as the extraction/transport interface: https://www.fao.org/4/v6530e/v6530e12.htm.

## Initial review findings (historical; superseded where stated below)

- Re-audit matched 14 English, Chinese and projected exchange cards, including Range role, bounds and evidence kind; targeted lint passed and no new Flow Set was used. Forest-retained tops, branches and felling losses are tracked in the stock/loss ledger, not as a waste exchange card.
- The initial review treated unchipped graded roundwood as the only final reference and forest chips as a non-reference process output. This interpretation is superseded by the conditional-output clarification below; it is retained here only as historical evidence.
- Other-grade outputs, rejects, shared assets and periods have explicit attribution and balance rules. The advisory rework/reject module remains unresolved because no rework loop is assumed; actual rejects have destinations and a separate flow-card identity requirement.
- Exact platform reference identity is unresolved, and broad `reasoned_estimate` ranges are provisional QA screens. The PCR remains `candidate`, not active or published.

## Initial decision (superseded by the 2026-10-10 classification correction)

Accept the exact CPC 3.0 `03112` edge to this material candidate PCR. The source leaf and PCR share the coniferous pulp/panel roundwood purpose, including the qualified direct-in-forest chip route and excluding mill residues. This classification acceptance does not approve unresolved flow identities or final TIDAS exchange construction.

## Current conditional-output clarification — 2026-10-09

This clarification aligns the decision record with the current bilingual methodology and generated conditional-output selector; it does not create a new mapping, alter the accepted CPC edge, or publish the candidate.

- Determine `forest_chip` activation from actual operation and dispatch records for each reference lot. Inactive selects `roadside_pulpwood`; active selects `direct_forest_chips`. Exactly one final output is selected per reference lot. Unknown activation or an unmatched lot is not an accepted default.
- Both states use 1 m3 solid under-bark wood equivalent at the existing forest-roadside gate. A mass or loose-chip observation requires matched lot-specific bark, moisture and conversion evidence; no universal density or chip yield is assumed.
- In an active chip lot, roundwood received by the chipper is a measured internal transfer, not a second final product or an invariant 1 m3 feed. Reconcile feed, chips, stock changes and separately identified rejects; do not count feed and chips twice as final output. Preserve product/period attribution and the unallocated physical ledger.
- Each selected output keeps its own semantic identity and independent UUID evidence. Do not reuse an unchipped anchor UUID as a chip identity. Both remain subject to candidate unresolved-identity restrictions.

The earlier statement that direct forest chips can never be the reference output is no longer current. Existing source-boundary evidence, exclusions, mapping acceptance metadata and the 28-card topology are retained; this update reconciles the decision explanation with the already implemented mutually exclusive terminal states.

## Current classification correction — 2026-10-10

Accept the CPC 03112 edge as `broader`, not `exact`. UNSD defines 03112 as coniferous roundwood for pulp and wood-based panels, while [CPC 31230](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Structure/Detail/EN/2100/31230) expressly includes wood deliberately reduced to chips from wood in the rough. It provides no forest-location exception. The FAO Joint Forest Sector Questionnaire aggregates direct forest chips with pulpwood for its statistics; that aggregation is not a CPC equivalence rule. The initial exact-acceptance rationale incorrectly transferred this statistical grouping into CPC classification.

Retain the existing classification-independent methodology, forest-roadside gate and conditional terminal selector. Its round/split pulpwood state covers CPC 03112; its additional direct-forest-chip state lies outside that leaf and must be classified by the actual chip form. This correction does not add a CPC 31230 mapping, claim coverage of all chips, split the PCR, change UUIDs or authorize publication. The broader edge requires consumers to check the selected terminal state rather than infer product classification from the PCR's incoming mapping. Unresolved identity and conversion evidence remains explicit candidate review debt.

## Current content-only handover representation — 2026-10-10

This supersedes the conditional machine selector above, not the product boundary or the accepted broader mapping. Both actual physical forms now terminate at one documentary producer-handover node with final card `pulpwood_handover`. Declare exactly one form and compatible concrete identity per lot; roundwood and chip receipt cards are mutually exclusive. Preserve measured processing/feed, rejects and stocks and the original gate; the handover creates no additional physical operation or burden. The form-specific unresolved UUIDs and chip conversion evidence remain required. This representation uses the target branch's existing projection contract without adding validator, Schema or module changes.
