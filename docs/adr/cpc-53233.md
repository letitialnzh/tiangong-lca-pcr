# CPC 3.0 53233: Dams

## Decision

Accept an **exact** classification edge from CPC 3.0 `53233` (Dams) to
`pcr.constructions-and-construction-services.constructions.dams`. This record documents the mapping acceptance already recorded at
`2026-09-29T02:02:21Z` by `codex-main-agent`; it does not backdate a new review or change
the target PCR's lifecycle state.

## Source leaf and semantic boundary

- Source leaf: `53233` — Dams, in the checked-in CPC 3.0 normalized leaves.
- Included product: Accepted dam body and integral foundation, seepage, spillway and outlet works.
- Exclusions: Stand-alone generation facilities, irrigation networks, ordinary flood channels and reservoir operation.
- Production route: Diversion and foundation preparation, then concrete placement or compacted earth/rock fill, appurtenant integration and testing.
- Product handover gate: Installed civil asset at signed handover.
- Reference product and measure: Completed dam body and declared integral appurtenances. One accepted dam; disclose crest length, height and structural volume separately.

These boundaries identify the same constructed asset named by the CPC leaf, not a sold
construction service, a manufactured component, or a later operating service. The
listed exclusions delimit adjacent CPC categories and separately measured assets.

## Evidence and readiness limit

The canonical manifest reciprocally names CPC 3.0 `53233` with an `exact` relation.
The English and Chinese PCR files and structured projection are present; the current
lifecycle pair is `candidate / authored_methodology` and Chinese translation is `aligned`.
The acceptance concerns classification identity only. Process-card, Range, Flow Set,
module-checklist and platform-flow verification remain governed by the PCR review and
validation contracts; this ADR does not certify publication readiness.

Reference-flow gap recorded by the PCR: No detail-confirmed generic completed-dam Product flow or support rows; reference UUIDs remain blank.
No incompatible candidate UUID is bound merely to complete this mapping.
