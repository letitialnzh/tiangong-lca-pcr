# CPC 3.0 53212: Railways

## Decision

Accept an **exact** classification edge from CPC 3.0 `53212` (Railways) to
`pcr.constructions-and-construction-services.constructions.railways`. This record documents the mapping acceptance already recorded at
`2026-09-23T09:34:36Z` by `codex-main-agent`; it does not backdate a new review or change
the target PCR's lifecycle state.

## Source leaf and semantic boundary

- Source leaf: `53212` — Railways, in the checked-in CPC 3.0 normalized leaves.
- Included product: Site-accepted railway alignment with formation, permanent way and declared trackside electrification, control and safety systems.
- Exclusions: Rolling stock and operation; station buildings; separately measured bridges, tunnels and utilities; future maintenance.
- Production route: Prepare formation; install track components; integrate in-scope power and safety systems; test and hand over.
- Product handover gate: Installed and accepted infrastructure at a named site.
- Reference product and measure: Site-accepted railway alignment segment. One route-km; report track-km separately for multiple tracks.

These boundaries identify the same constructed asset named by the CPC leaf, not a sold
construction service, a manufactured component, or a later operating service. The
listed exclusions delimit adjacent CPC categories and separately measured assets.

## Evidence and readiness limit

The canonical manifest reciprocally names CPC 3.0 `53212` with an `exact` relation.
The English and Chinese PCR files and structured projection are present; the current
lifecycle pair is `candidate / authored_methodology` and Chinese translation is `aligned`.
The acceptance concerns classification identity only. Process-card, Range, Flow Set,
module-checklist and platform-flow verification remain governed by the PCR review and
validation contracts; this ADR does not certify publication readiness.

Reference-flow gap recorded by the PCR: No confirmed Product-flow identity for a site-accepted railway alignment measured by route length; platform lookup was unavailable, so reference flow, property and unit-group UUIDs remain blank.
No incompatible candidate UUID is bound merely to complete this mapping.
