# CPC 3.0 53121: Industrial buildings

## Decision

Accept an **exact** classification edge from CPC 3.0 `53121` (Industrial buildings) to
`pcr.constructions-and-construction-services.constructions.industrial-buildings`. This record documents the mapping acceptance already recorded at
`2026-09-23T08:28:48Z` by `codex-main-agent`; it does not backdate a new review or change
the target PCR's lifecycle state.

## Source leaf and semantic boundary

- Source leaf: `53121` — Industrial buildings, in the checked-in CPC 3.0 normalized leaves.
- Included product: completed ordinary industrial factories/workshops and agricultural buildings including integrated silos
- Exclusions: general warehouses pending CPC 53122 decision; mining, power, chemical and specialized manufacturing works; separate production equipment
- Production route: site preparation and foundations; material/component transport; frame/envelope assembly; integrated services; commissioning and handover
- Product handover gate: as-built building ready for declared industrial or agricultural use
- Reference product and measure: Completed industrial building at site handover 1 m2 measured gross floor area; report total completed building area

These boundaries identify the same constructed asset named by the CPC leaf, not a sold
construction service, a manufactured component, or a later operating service. The
listed exclusions delimit adjacent CPC categories and separately measured assets.

## Evidence and readiness limit

The canonical manifest reciprocally names CPC 3.0 `53121` with an `exact` relation.
The English and Chinese PCR files and structured projection are present; the current
lifecycle pair is `candidate / authored_methodology` and Chinese translation is `aligned`.
The acceptance concerns classification identity only. Process-card, Range, Flow Set,
module-checklist and platform-flow verification remain governed by the PCR review and
validation contracts; this ADR does not certify publication readiness.
