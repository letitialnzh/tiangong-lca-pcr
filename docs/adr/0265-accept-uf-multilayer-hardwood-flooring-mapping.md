# Accept UF-bonded multilayer hardwood flooring panels

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `31626` to `pcr.other-transportable-goods-except-metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.products-of-wood-cork-straw-and-plaiting-materials.assembled-flooring-panels-made-out-of-wood`.

The method covers assembled multilayer flooring manufactured entirely from purchased dry non-tropical hardwood veneers, with UF cross-lamination, machining and a declared clear factory UV finish. It excludes single-layer solid flooring panels, HDF decorative laminate, real-wood-faced HDF cores and unassembled strips. CPC 3.0 covers assembled wooden flooring panels; the all-hardwood-veneer/UF/UV route is a narrower subset. Finished reference `a68f3cc0-8df3-4e20-8793-599f95e254a2` identifies the qualifying multilayer assembly, with net accepted panel mass and actual coating/moisture qualifiers.

Original CORRIM eastern-US engineered-flooring evidence supports process decomposition and distinguishes binder alternatives. The method selects the UF route and does not transplant the historical survey's numerical recipe, energy, density, emissions or plant coverage. NWFA and USDA originals supply applicable product and wood-measurement context. Installed flooring service, lifetime, installation loss and assumed structural equivalence are not manufactured-output defaults.

Coating-related purchased products remain separate actual formulations with supplier composition and fate accounting. A complete supplied coating background cannot be duplicated by adding its embedded ingredients as further purchases. Actual extra ingredients and cleaning agents must remain independently accounted where used. Steam/condensate enthalpy and a provider's gross-calorific-value reference remain a distinct compatibility question, not resolved by the existence of an MJ flow.

Its raw CAS is absent; actual supplier chemistry/SDS, coating grade and production route remain required. It is not silently replaced by a fused or sintered grade. Offcut and polyethylene-film records retain explicit source classification inconsistencies, bounded physical identities and recipient/provider checks. Unglued veneer rejects are segregated from UF-bonded panel trim.
