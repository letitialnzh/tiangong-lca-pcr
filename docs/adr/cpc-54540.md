## Reviewed semantic target

- Canonical PCR: `pcr.constructions-and-construction-services.construction-services.cast-in-place-building-concrete-placement-services`.
- Included product: Contractor placement, compaction, ordinary surface finishing, curing and acceptance of cast-in-place building elements.
- Excluded adjacent products/routes: Ready-mix manufacture; precast manufacture/erection; formwork fabrication/installation and reinforcement works; foundations engineering; roads, bridges, marine concrete; specialist coating systems; whole buildings.
- Actual route: Receive fresh concrete → place and compact → finish → protect and cure → inspect and accept.
- Starting condition: Accessible building workface; surveyed geometry and inspected, installed formwork/reinforcement/embedded items accepted by the responsible upstream package; delivered fresh concrete at site receipt.
- Handover/market state: Accepted installed construction service at contractor handover; no assumed lifetime service equivalence.
- Acceptance quality: Declared element, mixture/strength/exposure specification, geometry, finish, curing and acceptance evidence.
- Reference convention: 1 m3 accepted net in-place volume; One identified pour and its actual curing and acceptance period; no future repair cycles..

The relation is **narrower**: this stated product, starting condition, actual route and acceptance gate form one semantic subset of the official leaf.
