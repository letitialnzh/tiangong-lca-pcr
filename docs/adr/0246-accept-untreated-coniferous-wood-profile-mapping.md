# Untreated solid coniferous wood profiles

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `31211` to `pcr.other-transportable-goods-except-metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.products-of-wood-cork-straw-and-plaiting-materials.wood-continuously-shaped-along-any-of-its-edges-or-faces-including-strips-and-friezes-f-d3228e5f`. The supported product is unassembled, unglued, uncoated and unimpregnated coniferous solid wood with a continuous worked longitudinal profile, made from kiln-dried stock. Plain sawn or surfaced boards, engineered or finger-jointed products, assembled floors, doors/windows, bamboo and non-coniferous wood are excluded. The profile cross-section and botanical identity must be established before selection. This acceptance does not establish the whole classification leaf as methodologically covered.

The matching unpublished retained scaffold is promoted in place, preserving semantic identity. Its one-kg net finished-product reference excludes packaging and is tied to one terminal output. Stage measurements remain visible within the aggregated foreground unit; physical mass, water, carbon and internal-transfer ledgers remain separate from attributed environmental burdens. USDA Wood Handbook measurement and worked-profile context, CORRIM northern softwood upstream bookkeeping, and separate EPA sawmill and boiler source screening support bounded method choices. No regional survey yield, density, moisture, energy or emission value becomes a universal default. Profiling allocation additionally requires an actual joint step, comparable solid-wood coproducts and substantiated physical causality; the lumber survey does not directly validate it.
