# Conventional dry-process wood MDF

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `31441` to `pcr.other-transportable-goods-except-metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.products-of-wood-cork-straw-and-plaiting-materials.medium-density-fibreboard-mdf`. The method covers conventional uncoated wood MDF formed from refined fibres with the actual separately identified binder recipe, then hot pressed, cooled, sanded and trimmed. Dry process denotes forming rather than absence of water or steam. Wet hardboard, wet low-density fibreboard, nonwood panels, particleboard/OSB, coated panels and special preservative/fire-retardant formulations are excluded. Species/source mix, density, thickness, moisture and claimed performance require dataset evidence, not a universal threshold or legal limit.

Promote the corresponding unpublished retained scaffold. The reference Medium density fiberboard identity `57c184ac-a2d3-4500-af7b-80609ed89d2f` explicitly describes wood fibres, resin/wax and hot consolidation, with verified Mass/kg support. Reference output is one kg accepted net board at declared delivery moisture, excluding packaging. The aggregated line retains distinct refining, blend/drying, press and finishing state ledgers; conditional utility generation has equal physical supply/return links and no duplicate purchased heat burden.

USDA Wood Handbook, EPA's MDF guidance, separately scoped boiler evidence and CORRIM's North American 2021/2022 survey support qualitative route, collection and accounting requirements. Recipe, energy, density, emissions and whole-life scenarios are not transferred as defaults. Region and survey limitations remain explicit. Unverified resin formulations, steam states and wastes retain specific identity gaps, with no OSB-specific pMDI proxy or MF substitution for MUF.

The controller independently checked all 92 successful row searches, 71 adopted rows containing 33 distinct identities, and all 53 elementary air rows against raw state-100 records, official Chinese names, types, media and reference property/unit-group support. Twenty-one identity gaps remain. Seven additional rows have explicit quantity-compatibility review: wood-chip solid/bulk volume and supplier reference amount, three gas rows with meter/provider state correspondence, and three NOx rows with unproven monitoring/LCI/LCIA mass-basis alignment. A real NOx identity does not define NO2-equivalent mass, an NO/NO2 ratio or permission to include N2O or duplicate species. Unknown quantity conventions keep the affected link/result incomplete without fabricating values or withdrawing correctly identified flows.

The record remains an unpublished `candidate/authored_methodology`, Chinese author-aligned and scientific review pending. Author deterministic sync and complete measurement checks pass; full author validation stops at the expected obsolete shared alias binding. Shared rebuilding, consumer checks and full local validation are still required before handoff. The accepted association is bounded and establishes neither full-leaf applicability nor scientific approval or a completed empirical dataset.
