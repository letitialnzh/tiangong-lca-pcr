# CPC 3.0 53262: Power plants

## Decision

Accept an **exact** classification edge from CPC 3.0 `53262` (Power plants) to
`pcr.constructions-and-construction-services.constructions.power-plants`. This record documents the mapping acceptance already recorded at
`2026-09-29T04:17:39Z` by `codex-main-agent`; it does not backdate a new review or change
the target PCR's lifecycle state.

## Source leaf and semantic boundary

- Source leaf: `53262` — Power plants, in the checked-in CPC 3.0 normalized leaves.
- Included product: Accepted generating plant with integrated civil, generation and balance-of-plant systems inside one contract boundary.
- Exclusions: Electricity output and routine operation; independently contracted transmission; equipment-only sales; separate fuel-supply facilities.
- Production route: Site removal and preparation; foundation and civil forming; equipment and electrical integration; commissioning tests and defect closeout to signed handover.
- Product handover gate: Installed and accepted capital asset, not electricity supplied to the grid.
- Reference product and measure: Constructed and accepted electricity-generating plant. One accepted plant; report rated capacity separately in MW.

These boundaries identify the same constructed asset named by the CPC leaf, not a sold
construction service, a manufactured component, or a later operating service. The
listed exclusions delimit adjacent CPC categories and separately measured assets.

## Evidence and readiness limit

The canonical manifest reciprocally names CPC 3.0 `53262` with an `exact` relation.
The English and Chinese PCR files and structured projection are present; the current
lifecycle pair is `candidate / authored_methodology` and Chinese translation is `aligned`.
The acceptance concerns classification identity only. Process-card, Range, Flow Set,
module-checklist and platform-flow verification remain governed by the PCR review and
validation contracts; this ADR does not certify publication readiness.

Reference-flow gap recorded by the PCR: No detail-confirmed, count-compatible Product flow for an accepted whole power plant. Technology-specific coal, hydro, nuclear and wind candidates do not establish a generic asset identity.
No incompatible candidate UUID is bound merely to complete this mapping.
