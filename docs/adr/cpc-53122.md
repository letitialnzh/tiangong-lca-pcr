# CPC 3.0 53122: Commercial buildings

## Decision

Accept an **exact** classification edge from CPC 3.0 `53122` (Commercial buildings) to
`pcr.constructions-and-construction-services.constructions.commercial-buildings`. This record documents the mapping acceptance already recorded at

the target PCR's lifecycle state.

## Source leaf and semantic boundary

- Source leaf: `53122` — Commercial buildings, in the checked-in CPC 3.0 normalized leaves.
- Included product: completed retail, warehouse, exhibition, office, transport-terminal, parking-garage and service-station buildings
- Exclusions: industrial/other non-residential buildings; separate plant, vehicles and equipment; land and operation
- Production route: supplied components and freight; site/foundation works; shell assembly; fixed-system integration, tests and handover
- Product handover gate: as-built building ready for declared commercial use at site handover
- Reference product and measure: Completed commercial building at accepted site handover 1 m2 gross floor area; retain whole-building area and inventory

These boundaries identify the same constructed asset named by the CPC leaf, not a sold
construction service, a manufactured component, or a later operating service. The
listed exclusions delimit adjacent CPC categories and separately measured assets.
