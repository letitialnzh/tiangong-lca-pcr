# CPC 03232 — accepted narrower mapping

The official CPC 3.0 note identifies untended gathered edible mushrooms and excludes intentionally grown or managed/cultivated production. The proposed PCR covers only fresh/chilled untended fruiting bodies, with truffles excluded, through declared gatherer or primary-producer dispatch after actual simple handling. Dried, dehydrated, frozen, cooked, salted, canned or other processed goods are outside this method. The official note does not establish that the entire leaf is exclusively fresh-state output, so do not assert exact whole-leaf equivalence.

Source: https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Structure/Detail/EN/2100/03232 . Distinct gathered/cultivated and fresh/dried contexts: https://www.fao.org/4/y5489e/y5489e08.htm . Scope review must verify wild-source records, species/edible qualification, physical state, actual route and producer gate. Edible qualification is a documented product-identity requirement, not food-safety certification or permission to eat unidentified fungi.

Author recommendation was narrower partial coverage only. Main has independently accepted that semantic relation;
unresolved UUIDs limit readiness and never justify a false broader identity.

## Main decision

Accept the individual narrower edge for fresh/chilled untended gathered qualified fruiting bodies at the actual
gatherer/primary-producer dispatch. Exclude truffles, cultivation/enhancement and dried/frozen/processed states;
the official note does not prove whole-leaf fresh-only equivalence. Main reviewed all thirty-five cards,
EN/ZH/structured quantity and Range fields, actual loss/soil/water/stock ownership and all twelve selected modules.
Compatible water-vapour/Mass evidence was reused; cultivated/dehydrated/truffle and incompatible generic biomass
candidates were not forced into UUID fields. Final other qualified goods have a real product role, not a waste
surrogate. Target check: complete, 179 performed, zero skipped. Final reference UUID remains unresolved.
Lifecycle, three-target repository validation and coverage readback are recorded in
builder/research-sessions/cpc-03-round4/main-review.md. Candidate consistency is not publication or concrete-exchange readiness.
