# Accept an uncoated solid hardwood door leaf association

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `31622` to `pcr.other-transportable-goods-except-metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.products-of-wood-cork-straw-and-plaiting-materials.doors-and-their-frames-and-thresholds-made-out-of-wood`.

The method covers one complete interior door leaf assembled from purchased kiln-dried planed solid hardwood with mortise-and-tenon joinery and supplied one-part aqueous PVAc adhesive cured at ambient conditions. It excludes a frame, threshold, hardware, seals, glazing and a complete doorset; veneered or engineered-core doors and applied coatings are outside this method. CPC 3.0 includes doors and their frames and thresholds; the complete door leaf is a narrower physical subset, not evidence of coverage of every item in that class.

The broad finished wooden-door reference includes the qualified leaf, with accepted net whole-leaf mass at recorded moisture as denominator. No standard per-door weight or generic density is supplied. USDA Wood Handbook chapter 10 PVAc tables and bonding discussion support the adhesive family and supplied-mixture accounting, while the acquired solid-wood door product description supports the solid, unfinished, mortise-and-tenon slab anchor. A separate construction guide is used only for qualitative acceptance and packing, not as an engineered-core recipe or tolerance source.

Independent direct-record review found that the original adhesive candidate's acetate name conflicted with CAS 9002-89-5. The repair adopts PVAc `46c91184-28ea-4b2e-a4e7-c07f3f84369b`, CAS 9003-20-7, whose state-100 description includes aqueous white-glue emulsion, with a verified Mass/kg chain. The PCR records one as-supplied mixture and requires actual polymer fraction, total solids, water/additives and supplier SDS, including correspondence between wet or dry background reference quantities. Embedded ingredients are not duplicated as separate purchases; this route adds no separate catalyst or dilution water. Unknown supplier quantities remain collection requirements, not invented defaults.
