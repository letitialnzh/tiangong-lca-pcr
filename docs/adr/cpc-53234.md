# CPC 3.0 53234: Irrigation and flood control waterworks

`pcr.constructions-and-construction-services.constructions.irrigation-and-flood-control-waterworks`. This record documents the mapping acceptance already recorded at

the target PCR's lifecycle state.

## Source leaf and semantic boundary

- Source leaf: `53234` — Irrigation and flood control waterworks, in the checked-in CPC 3.0 normalized leaves.
- Included product: Accepted irrigation channels and structures or flood-control channels, levees and integral works.
- Exclusions: Water-supply conduits, dams, navigation channels, stand-alone pipelines, farming, operation and maintenance.
- Production route: Survey and excavation; canal/levee formation with route-specific lining or compacted fill; integration of controls, drainage and protection; testing and handover.
- Product handover gate: Installed civil asset at signed construction acceptance.
- Reference product and measure: Constructed irrigation or flood-control waterwork with declared function and integral elements. One site-accepted asset; report functional length, formed volume and liner or protected area separately.

These boundaries identify the same constructed asset named by the CPC leaf, not a sold
construction service, a manufactured component, or a later operating service. The
listed exclusions delimit adjacent CPC categories and separately measured assets.
