# Accepted CPC 03220 mapping

Official CPC 3.0 category is Natural cork, raw or simply prepared. Proposed semantic target covers raw harvested or simply cleaned/rested/boiled natural cork at an actually declared primary producer handover. Species, virgin/secondary/reproduction harvest, actual preparation, unremoved back and grade are lot qualifiers, not manufactured-form equivalence.

Exclusions distinguish CPC 31921 debacked/roughly squared/shaped/granulated/ground and waste cork, and 31922 finished articles/agglomerates. The target explicitly owns only actual source and primary preparation: recurrent standing-tree peeling, evidenced coppice wood/bark interface, or upstream already-produced purchase, with conditional operations and no default timber felling.

Accepted relation is exact for this raw/simple semantic scope after independent main-agent review. It does not establish publication readiness. Unresolved UUIDs remain candidate review evidence.

Sources: UNSD official CPC3.0 explanatory-notes PDF sections 03220, 31921 and 31922; FAO cork chapter; APCOR transformation process as cited in canonical PCR.
