- Accepted relation: `exact`.

## Semantic boundary

- Included: unprocessed coniferous industrial roundwood intended for poles, piling, posts, fencing, pitprops, shingles or shakes, wood wool, tanning, distillation, shiitake cultivation, match blocks, or comparable non-sawlog, non-veneer, non-pulpwood use.
- Excluded: sawlogs and veneer logs, pulpwood and panel wood, wood fuel, processed poles or posts, sawnwood, and downstream treated/fabricated goods.
- Production route: managed stand or explicitly declared recovered standing source; felling and extraction; bucking and log assortment; forest-roadside landing handover before long-distance haulage or downstream processing.
- Quantitative distinction: the verified platform flow is Mass-based, so the PCR reference amount is 1 kg as received. FAO forest-product statistics use under-bark solid m³; it must be reported separately with lot-specific measurement and conversion evidence.
