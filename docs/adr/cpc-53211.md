## Source leaf and semantic boundary

- Source leaf: `53211` — Highways (except elevated highways), streets and roads, in the checked-in CPC 3.0 normalized leaves.
- Included product: Non-elevated highways, streets and roads; surfaced parking, driveways, pedestrian and bicycle ways; integral safety installations and declared underpasses/overpasses
- Exclusions: Elevated highways, highway tunnels, standalone bridges, railway tracks, runways, paving products, construction services, road use and later maintenance
- Production route: Earthwork and compacted formation, pavement-layer assembly, finishing and handover. A rigid-concrete alternative replaces the asphalt surface route for the same area and requires changed materials, equipment, curing, waste and tests.
- Product handover gate: Passable accepted civil asset at the site; traffic use and design-life scenarios are separate
- Reference product and measure: Completed non-elevated road or surfaced-way segment at the declared site One accepted segment; also report centreline length in m and paved area in m2 for intensity

These boundaries identify the same constructed asset named by the CPC leaf, not a sold
construction service, a manufactured component, or a later operating service. The
listed exclusions delimit adjacent CPC categories and separately measured assets.
