# Accept a bounded random-wafer wood board association

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `31439` to `pcr.other-transportable-goods-except-metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.products-of-wood-cork-straw-and-plaiting-materials.other-particle-boards-of-wood-e-g-waferboard`.

The method covers uncoated, unpreserved broad-thin wood waferboard with random layup, powdered uncured PF resole, indirect externally supplied steam heating, electric handling and dry dust collection. Deliberately oriented OSB/waferboard, fine-particle UF board, direct-fired dryers, onsite combustion, other binders, overlays and wet controls require a separate review. The unpublished retained scaffold matches this semantic subset. Official CPC 3.0 explicitly names waferboard in its residual wood-particleboard subclass.

EPA AP-42 10.6.1 (2002) supports random wafer forming, indirect conveyor drying and thermal PF bonding. USDA FPL-GTR-53 (1986), Figure 2, and FPL-RP-574 (1999), Table 1, establish historical wide-wafer/commercial product anchors, not the exact recipe, species or steam supply of a contemporary plant. Wood Handbook moisture rules and WRI/WBCSD's 2011 allocation chapter are used within their stated scopes. Historical strengths, dimensions, emissions and process settings are not transplanted as defaults.

The finished manufactured reference `dc4d3259-45c1-4d98-8264-35a90058e65c` has a verified Mass/kg chain and includes the physical subset.
