# CPC 03239 — broad category, not the former narrow subset

## Driving decision

The user explicitly selected full methodology expansion rather than an identity-only rename. The former unmerged wild-edible-leafy-vegetables candidate is replaced by authored methodology at the retained broad identity `pcr.agriculture-forestry-and-fishery-products.forestry-and-logging-products.other-wild-edible-edible-products-n-e-c`. There is no published release or immutable history to rewrite. Prior narrower-scope validation/search conclusions are historical and do not certify this expanded method.

## Semantic coverage

This PCR covers otherwise unclassified edible products obtained by gathering from untended sources, not only leafy vegetables. It accommodates eligible leaves, shoots, stems, flowers, roots/tubers, non-berry fruits, seeds and other edible parts or products only after exclusion of more specific classifications. Those examples are not automatic classification decisions or interchangeable foods. Record the actual edible species/part, wild-source status, incoming state, acceptance specification and primary producer/gatherer handover.

Exclude deliberately grown or managed edible production, wild nuts/mushrooms/truffles/berries assigned to their own subclasses, animal/fishery products and any other goods assigned elsewhere, and cooked, fermented, extracted or other processed food classified outside this primary category. Do not include a sap, spice, medicinal product or seed simply because it can be eaten; review its specific class first. Commercial preservation that changes classification requires another applicable PCR rather than silently extending this one.

- `wild_aboveground_parts` — Eligible leaves, shoots, stems and flowers: Identify the actually marketed edible part and whether its acceptance includes stalks or other attached parts. Collect seasonal gathering, trimming and actual fresh protection; do not assume every species is ready to eat.
- `wild_belowground_parts` — Eligible roots, tubers and below-ground parts: Activate digging, soil separation, washing and peeling only when performed. Keep removed edible biomass and incidental soil in separate ledgers; measure wet soil/washwater transfers and dry-matter losses without inventing soil-emission factors.
- `wild_other_food` — Eligible non-berry fruits, seeds and other residual wild foods: Verify residual-class eligibility and edible fraction. Record actual shell/peel/seed separation, ripeness and grade; specific wild nuts or berries retain their own class. A marketed whole food is weighed whole when its acceptance basis includes the peel/shell; report edible fraction separately, not as an unannounced denominator change.

## Method and reference

Accepted net mass of the declared primary food in its actual accepted state, excluding transport packaging, soil, free washwater and rejected matter. Keep accepted peel/shell only when sold as part of the declared whole-food state, with edible fraction disclosed. Do not normalize a whole-food inventory by an edible-only denominator without an explicit separate conversion. The common reference is 1 kg at the actual primary producer handover, with state-specific measured quantities and declared treatment/composition; no equivalence across different products is inferred. Product components are distinguished from transport packaging. Owned source production/collection, primary preparation, eligible actual treatment/holding and final dispatch have explicit responsibility, bypass, interface, loss, stock and attribution rules. Management is excluded for deliberately grown or managed food.
