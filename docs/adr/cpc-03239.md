## Semantic boundary

Included: fresh raw eligible wild vegetable leaves and declared leafy shoots from untended terrestrial sources at actual primary gatherer/preparer handover; 1 kg accepted net fresh mass with species, intended parts, source, grade, moisture and gate evidence.

Exclude nuts, mushrooms, truffles, berries and all more explicitly classified goods; roots/tubers, saps/gums, insects, animal products, aquatic algae, medicinal-only/spice-only/ornamental material; intentionally cultivated or agriculturally managed leaves; cooked, blanched, dried, frozen, fermented and extracted states. Raw means market state, not ready-to-eat safety. Later safe-use preparation is declared downstream.

Routes: actual own gathering or already gathered receipt and transfer; conditional initial trimming/cleaning/grading, conditional fresh holding/cooling, actual packaging and mandatory primary net acceptance. Natural-source removal and capture share one actual physical gathering ownership, without duplicated nodes or purchased-feed removal. Source/season/trip, unsuccessful trips, source composition, stock periods, intended output sets and shared tools/transfer/holding attribution create material methodology needs distinct from cultivated leafy crop cycles. Existing cultivated-leafy material is not a same semantic category; the broad miscellaneous-wild-food scaffold is untouched.

This narrower edge would mean accepted methodology exists for the above subset, **not completion of all miscellaneous wild food** or assertion that all species/parts are legally or safely interchangeable.
