# CPC 3.0 53213: Airfield runways

## Decision

Accept an **exact** classification edge from CPC 3.0 `53213` (Airfield runways) to
`pcr.constructions-and-construction-services.constructions.airfield-runways`. This record documents the mapping acceptance already recorded at
`2026-09-28T05:09:36Z` by `codex-main-agent`; it does not backdate a new review or change
the target PCR's lifecycle state.

## Source leaf and semantic boundary

- Source leaf: `53213` — Airfield runways, in the checked-in CPC 3.0 normalized leaves.
- Included product: Site-accepted runway, taxiway or apron pavement and contract-integral non-building structures.
- Exclusions: Airport buildings, aircraft, operations, later maintenance and separately measured lighting or navigation assets.
- Production route: Prepare subgrade and drainage; place designed base and flexible or rigid surface; finish and test.
- Product handover gate: Installed and accepted airside infrastructure at a specified site.
- Reference product and measure: Site-accepted airfield pavement asset. 1 m2 of accepted plan-view paved area; also report total continuous-segment area.

These boundaries identify the same constructed asset named by the CPC leaf, not a sold
construction service, a manufactured component, or a later operating service. The
listed exclusions delimit adjacent CPC categories and separately measured assets.
