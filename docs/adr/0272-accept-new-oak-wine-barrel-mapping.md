# Accept new oak wine barrels with permanent steel hoops

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `31703` to `pcr.other-transportable-goods-except-metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.products-of-wood-cork-straw-and-plaiting-materials.casks-barrels-vats-tubs-and-other-coopers-products-and-parts-thereof-of-wood-including-staves`.

The method covers complete new all-oak wine barrels with permanent steel hoops, from qualified oak blanks through shell and head manufacture, bending, controlled sensory toasting and final leak testing. It distinguishes steam or open-fire bending from toasting, and excludes stave-only delivery, other woods, composites, vats, charred spirits barrels and used or refurbished barrels. Actual barrel specification, moisture, wood and permanent-metal quantities determine net complete barrel mass; no standard volume, per-barrel weight, toast energy, lifetime or wine-aging performance is invented.
