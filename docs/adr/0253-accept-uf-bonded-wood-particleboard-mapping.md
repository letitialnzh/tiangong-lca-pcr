# Wood particleboard with audited UF preparation

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `31431` to `pcr.other-transportable-goods-except-metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.products-of-wood-cork-straw-and-plaiting-materials.particle-board-of-wood`. The method covers flat, non-oriented, uncoated wood particleboard made by dry forming and hot pressing with unmodified UF binder. Its foreground explicitly expands actual UF preparation at the board site or an identified supplier with ingredient, yield, heat and emission records. Purchased aqueous formaldehyde starts upstream of that reactor; methanol-to-formaldehyde manufacture remains in linked supply. A generic purchased binder family cannot replace the required batch evidence. Each species/source blend, particle architecture, recipe, density/thickness and press route forms its own measured cohort.

The matching unpublished scaffold is promoted without changing published content. The reference particle board identity `4f19ca17-7b3b-11dd-ad8b-0800200c9a66` has verified Mass/kg support. The unit is one kg accepted net panel at release moisture, excluding packaging. OSB/waferboard, nonwood/fibre/faced/moulded panels, treated recovered furnish, other binders and unidentified wax formulations are excluded. This is not full classification coverage or a building-service equivalence claim.

USDA and EPA support board/process distinctions; CORRIM's North American 2021/2022 survey supports bounded source, recovery, process and allocation questions. Wilson's US 2005 resin survey supports reaction/ingredient anatomy only, with its different formaldehyde-production starting gate explicitly excluded from numerical transfer. No survey-average density, recipe, yield, emissions, energy or downstream lifetime becomes a default. Physical supplier-resin transfers, recovered-wood and internal-fuel ledgers remain separate from allocated burdens.

The controller independently checked 51 successful row queries, 35 adopted rows with 33 distinct identities, and all 20 elementary air rows. Raw state-100 identities, official Chinese names, flow type/medium and reference property/unit-group chains match. Sixteen rows retain specific unresolved identities. Review removed a nominal ammonium-chloride match whose absorption-tower state did not establish the delivered catalyst identity. Steel-band use is conditional on its actual composition and reprocessing route. Wood-chip Volume identity does not establish solid/bulk convention; actual supplier reference amount, measured packing/moisture/state and conversion evidence are required. Natural-gas site and supplier volume bases must also match. Identity success alone does not complete background quantity linkage.

The record remains an unpublished `candidate/authored_methodology`, Chinese author-aligned and scientifically pending. Deterministic sync and complete measurement checks pass; the author's full validation stops at stale shared aliases. Serial shared-artifact rebuilding, consumer checks and full local validation remain required before handoff. Acceptance is a bounded mapping decision, not scientific approval, publication or completion of an empirical dataset.
