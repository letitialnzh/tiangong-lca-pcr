# Accept a bounded wood lattice cellular board association

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `31610` to `pcr.other-transportable-goods-except-metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.products-of-wood-cork-straw-and-plaiting-materials.cellular-board`.

The method covers dry purchased coniferous spaced parallel laths or a wood lattice, non-tropical hardwood veneer skins, onsite preparation of powdered UF adhesive and electric hot pressing into uncoated interior cellular boards. It excludes continuous solid-strip blockboard, all-veneer plywood, paper or aluminium honeycomb and complete doors. The official CPC 31610 inclusion of spaced parallel/lattice wood-core construction supports this narrower association. Cross-references in 3141/3142 do not establish additional accepted mappings.

The official UN classification originals and USDA Wood Handbook descriptions and adhesive table support the structural distinction and UF route. They provide no universal factory recipe, energy demand, density or moisture target. Finished cellular-board reference 4b4e8668-1076-4cb6-ac5c-2334d535b1fb has been directly verified, with a Mass/kg chain; actual core geometry, skin material, bond recipe, dispatch state and net accepted panel mass remain required.

All 24 rows were searched. After independent correction, 17 rows retain 17 verified identities, including all six elementary rows, and seven remain specifically unresolved. A generic-named wastewater UUID was rejected because its original route explicitly specifies electrolytic manganese slag washing; the actual combined UF preparation/equipment-cleaning wastewater remains a concrete empty-UUID row. Corrugated packaging retains its verified identity only for actual C/E/F flute, at least 80% fibre, recycled-containing multilayer recyclable board; these identity limits are not recipe defaults or recycling credits. Physical balances precede allocation, and the complete measurement check performs 123 checks with none skipped.

Independent review checked raw state-100 identities, exact official English/Chinese names, flow types, media, chemical origins and the reference property-to-unit-group chain, as well as bilingual measurement and original source applicability. Identity verification remains distinct from actual quantity and supplier/background compatibility. The unpublished method remains `candidate/authored_methodology`, author-aligned in Chinese and pending independent scientific review. This association does not establish full classification coverage, publication or empirical dataset completeness.
