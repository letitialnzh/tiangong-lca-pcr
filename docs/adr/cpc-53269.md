# CPC 3.0 53269: Other constructions for manufacturing

## Decision

Accept an **exact** classification edge from CPC 3.0 `53269` (Other constructions for manufacturing) to
`pcr.constructions-and-construction-services.constructions.other-constructions-for-manufacturing`. This record documents the mapping acceptance already recorded at
`2026-09-29T04:46:50Z` by `codex-main-agent`; it does not backdate a new review or change
the target PCR's lifecycle state.

## Source leaf and semantic boundary

- Source leaf: `53269` — Other constructions for manufacturing, in the checked-in CPC 3.0 normalized leaves.
- Included product: Completed product-specialized manufacturing facilities with integrated route-specific structures and equipment.
- Exclusions: Generic industrial buildings; equipment-only sales; manufactured output; operation; independently delivered infrastructure.
- Production route: Site removal/preparation; foundation and process-structure forming; equipment/utility/control integration; tests, correction and handover.
- Product handover gate: Installed and accepted capital asset, not manufacturing service or commodity output.
- Reference product and measure: Constructed and accepted product-specialized manufacturing facility. One accepted facility; designed throughput is a qualifier, not the reference amount.

These boundaries identify the same constructed asset named by the CPC leaf, not a sold
construction service, a manufactured component, or a later operating service. The
listed exclusions delimit adjacent CPC categories and separately measured assets.

## Evidence and readiness limit

The canonical manifest reciprocally names CPC 3.0 `53269` with an `exact` relation.
The English and Chinese PCR files and structured projection are present; the current
lifecycle pair is `candidate / authored_methodology` and Chinese translation is `aligned`.
The acceptance concerns classification identity only. Process-card, Range, Flow Set,
module-checklist and platform-flow verification remain governed by the PCR review and
validation contracts; this ADR does not certify publication readiness.

Reference-flow gap recorded by the PCR: No detail-confirmed count-compatible Product flow for one accepted specialized manufacturing facility; process-family labels are not interchangeable with a whole-asset identity.
No incompatible candidate UUID is bound merely to complete this mapping.
