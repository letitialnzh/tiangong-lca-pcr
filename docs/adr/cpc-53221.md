# CPC 3.0 53221: Bridges and elevated highways

## Decision

Accept an **exact** classification edge from CPC 3.0 `53221` (Bridges and elevated highways) to
`pcr.constructions-and-construction-services.constructions.bridges-and-elevated-highways`. This record documents the mapping acceptance already recorded at
`2026-09-28T05:09:36Z` by `codex-main-agent`; it does not backdate a new review or change
the target PCR's lifecycle state.

## Source leaf and semantic boundary

- Source leaf: `53221` — Bridges and elevated highways, in the checked-in CPC 3.0 normalized leaves.
- Included product: Site-accepted bridges, viaducts and elevated highways of declared duty.
- Exclusions: At-grade roads, tunnels, isolated paving or repair, operation and future maintenance.
- Production route: Form foundations and supports; cast in place or erect verified prefabricated elements; integrate deck, connections and safety systems; test and hand over.
- Product handover gate: Installed and accepted at the project site.
- Reference product and measure: Complete site-accepted bridge or elevated-highway structure. One accepted asset; structural length, deck area and span count are mandatory size descriptors.

These boundaries identify the same constructed asset named by the CPC leaf, not a sold
construction service, a manufactured component, or a later operating service. The
listed exclusions delimit adjacent CPC categories and separately measured assets.
