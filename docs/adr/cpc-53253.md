Accept an **exact** classification edge from CPC 3.0 `53253` (Sewage and water treatment plants) to
`pcr.constructions-and-construction-services.constructions.sewage-and-water-treatment-plants`. This record documents the mapping acceptance already recorded at

## Source leaf and semantic boundary

- Source leaf: `53253` — Sewage and water treatment plants, in the checked-in CPC 3.0 normalized leaves.
- Included product: Accepted treatment plant or separately contracted sewage-system facility and integral contract connections.
- Exclusions: Independently accepted local mains, long-distance pipelines, equipment-only sales, routine treatment service.
- Production route: Site excavation; civil tank/foundation forming; equipment, process-piping and controls integration; dry/wet tests, remediation and signed handover.
- Product handover gate: Installed asset at engineering acceptance, not a volume of treated water.
- Reference product and measure: Constructed and accepted sewage- or water-treatment plant or contracted sewage-system facility. One accepted facility; report design capacity in m3/day and connection dimensions separately.

These boundaries identify the same constructed asset named by the CPC leaf, not a sold
construction service, a manufactured component, or a later operating service. The
listed exclusions delimit adjacent CPC categories and separately measured assets.
