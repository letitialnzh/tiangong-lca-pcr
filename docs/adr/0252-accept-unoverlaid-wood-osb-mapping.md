# Unoverlaid resin-bonded wood OSB

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `31432` to `pcr.other-transportable-goods-except-metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.products-of-wood-cork-straw-and-plaiting-materials.oriented-strand-board-osb`. The method covers unoverlaid wood OSB with long strands, deliberately oriented face layers and actual declared core orientation, layer geometry and PF/pMDI formulations. It excludes random particleboard/waferboard, fibre/veneer panels, nonwood panels, overlays, preservative/fire-retardant formulations and unreviewed binder chemistry. Layer counts and grade/service expectations are not inferred from a classification label or one survey.

The unpublished retained scaffold is promoted in place. The reference product `3291d1cf-a318-4271-9435-39534fa45e67` is a manufactured finished OSB identity with a verified Mass/kg chain. The declared reference is one kg accepted net board at actual dispatch moisture, excluding packaging; it is not a functional equivalence claim across building grades.

USDA Wood Handbook, EPA AP-42 10.6.1, a separately scoped wood-boiler source and CORRIM's US 2022/2023 survey support the route and collection questions. CORRIM repeated particle/species labels are not copied into numeric factors. Raw logs retain starting-gate choices, layer recipes, stage ownership, internal heat and physical transfers, post-control emissions and separate burden allocation. Actual supplier pMDI rates and fuel calorific values are required rather than database comment defaults. Steel-band identity is conditional on matching composition and the specified recycling route.

The controller independently checked all 52 queried rows, 30 adopted identities and all 20 elementary rows against state-100 direct records, official Chinese names, types, media and property/unit-group support. Twenty-two rows retain specific unresolved identities. Review corrected a real natural-gas property-label error: the selected flow is Volume/m3, not Mass. Site and supplier volume states, absolute pressure/temperature, moisture/composition and compressibility must match or have a traceable conversion. OIML engineering correspondence supports the conversion; no current legal-compliance or universal standard-condition claim is made. Unknown provider basis leaves background linkage incomplete. Purchased steam identity/net-heat compatibility remains unresolved, and a broad wood-packaging collection was replaced by a concrete unresolved dunnage row.

The record remains unpublished `candidate/authored_methodology`, with author-aligned Chinese and scientific review pending. The accepted association is bounded and does not establish full-leaf applicability, scientific approval or a complete measured dataset. Deterministic author sync and complete measurement checks pass. Serial shared-artifact rebuilding, consumer checks and full local validation remain required before handoff.
