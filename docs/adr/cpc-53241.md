# CPC 3.0 53241: Long-distance pipelines

## Decision

Accept an **exact** classification edge from CPC 3.0 `53241` (Long-distance pipelines) to
`pcr.constructions-and-construction-services.constructions.long-distance-pipelines`. This record documents the mapping acceptance already recorded at
`2026-09-29T03:08:58Z` by `codex-main-agent`; it does not backdate a new review or change
the target PCR's lifecycle state.

## Source leaf and semantic boundary

- Source leaf: `53241` — Long-distance pipelines, in the checked-in CPC 3.0 normalized leaves.
- Included product: Accepted long-distance transmission pipeline and integral pumping structures in the project scope.
- Exclusions: Local distribution mains, aqueducts, separate station projects and pipeline transport service.
- Production route: Corridor preparation and trenching, pipe stringing and welding, field coating, laying/backfill or crossing, pressure testing, restoration and handover.
- Product handover gate: Installed civil asset at signed handover.
- Reference product and measure: Completed long-distance pipeline asset with declared integral stations. One accepted project; disclose accepted route length and installed pipe mass separately.

These boundaries identify the same constructed asset named by the CPC leaf, not a sold
construction service, a manufactured component, or a later operating service. The
listed exclusions delimit adjacent CPC categories and separately measured assets.

## Evidence and readiness limit

The canonical manifest reciprocally names CPC 3.0 `53241` with an `exact` relation.
The English and Chinese PCR files and structured projection are present; the current
lifecycle pair is `candidate / authored_methodology` and Chinese translation is `aligned`.
The acceptance concerns classification identity only. Process-card, Range, Flow Set,
module-checklist and platform-flow verification remain governed by the PCR review and
validation contracts; this ADR does not certify publication readiness.

Reference-flow gap recorded by the PCR: No detail-confirmed Product flow represents one accepted long-distance pipeline asset across conveyed media, alignments and integral pumping stations. Reference flow, property and unit-group UUIDs remain blank; a pipeline-transportation service candidate is not the asset.
No incompatible candidate UUID is bound merely to complete this mapping.
