# Accepted semantic mapping decision

The material category is fresh whole edible berries gathered from proved untended sources and accepted at the actual primary gatherer/preparer handover, measured as 1 kg net fresh whole accepted berries. CPC 03234 has a broader state coverage; this edge is therefore proposed narrower, not exact or whole-class completion.

Exclude managed/cultivated berries even if marketed as wild (University of Maine describes managed commercial lowbush wild-blueberry cycles), jam, juice, pulp, dried/frozen/cooked or chemically preserved goods, nonfood/ornamental products, other wild foods and downstream retail gates. Stems/leaves, foreign matter, packaging tare and other grades are excluded from reference mass.

Owned source removal/gathering shares one node; purchased already gathered berries retain upstream burden without a second natural removal. Actual transfer, initial cleaning and grading, fresh holding/cooling and packaging/primary acceptance are independently declared responsibilities. A separate wild category is justified by untended-source proof, removal ownership, unsuccessful-trip/site-season attribution and fresh primary-gate duties, not by the classification code alone.

Evidence: UNSD CPC 3.0 official explanatory notes, subclass 03234 (PDF page 39 printed / index 38); University of Maine Cooperative Extension About the Maine Wild Blueberry; FAO Food security, nutrition and health, fresh perishability/state distinction. Exact source links are retained in the PCR Data Sources. No numerical values or safety assurances transferred from examples.

Main-agent final findings: EN/ZH/structured 29 cards are aligned, including actual incidental natural-source removal and direct return separately from technosphere waste handling. All cards have compatible provisional non-enforcing Range metadata; all 12 selected modules and 48 obligations were manually compared with completed product-specific duties. Target PCR check passes with 149 performed and zero skipped. Current candidate detail limits fresh untended berries to forest roadside and does not establish equivalence to every real preparation/holding/primary-handover gate; 29 identities remain explicitly unresolved. Mass/kg support is independently read and compatible, not proof of berry identity.

Accept a narrower positive edge for this reviewed material methodology. This does not finish every CPC 03234 state, certify concrete exchanges, activate or publish the PCR.
