# CPC 03249 — broad category, not the former narrow subset

## Driving decision

The user explicitly selected full methodology expansion rather than an identity-only rename. The former unmerged fresh-forest-ornamental-foliage candidate is replaced by authored methodology at the retained broad identity `pcr.agriculture-forestry-and-fishery-products.forestry-and-logging-products.other-parts-of-plants-without-flowers-or-flower-buds-and-grasses-mosses-and-lichens-sui-ae228e3b`. There is no published release or immutable history to rewrite. Prior narrower-scope validation/search conclusions are historical and do not certify this expanded method.

## Semantic coverage

This PCR covers the ornamental-material category, not only fresh wild foliage. It includes ornamental mosses and lichens; flowerless branches, foliage, grasses, leaves and other plant parts; the Proteaceae and cereal-ear entries identified by the classification; and baskets, wreaths, buttonholes, bouquets, posies and similar compositions made from the included materials. Fresh, naturally dry, dried, dyed, bleached, impregnated and other prepared states are included when their actual composition and classification remain compatible. Wild and managed sources are both possible; ornamental use does not prove wild origin.

Exclude Christmas trees, plants for planting/propagation assigned to another class, cut flowers/flower buds and compositions classified as flower goods, artificial vegetation, and manufactured goods outside the ornamental-material class. Classify mixed flower/non-flower compositions from their actual composition and applicable classification rules; do not force them here because some foliage is present. Listing Proteaceae or cereal ears is not authority to relabel otherwise excluded flower goods.

- `ornamental_foliage` — Branches, foliage, leaves and eligible other plant parts: Declare species/part, wild or managed source and fresh/dry/treated state. Record actual cutting, cleaning, grading and preservation; no fixed salal recipe or freshness requirement applies to every material.
- `ornamental_grasses` — Ornamental grasses and eligible cereal ears: Declare maturity, seed retention, harvested part and dry-matter/moisture basis. Distinguish intentionally sold ears or seeds from incidental shedding; do not count retained seeds twice.
- `ornamental_moss_lichen` — Ornamental mosses and lichens: Identify material and substrate. Measure attached bark, soil or stone separately and establish whether it is an intentionally retained component of an eligible accepted composition or incidental matter excluded from reference mass. Declare collection, moisture and actual stabilization. Do not assume tree growth, standard regeneration, carbon credits or removal of the entire substrate.
- `ornamental_composition` — Eligible ornamental compositions: Activate assembly for actual compositions. Identify each botanical input and structural/fastening component, its supplier coverage and retained mass. A product frame or permanent fastener is not transport packaging; botanical content and whole-article mass must both be disclosed.

## Method and reference

Accepted net mass of the declared ornamental good in its actual delivered state. Include retained treatment ingredients and permanent components of an eligible composition; exclude detachable transport packaging, incidental soil/stone, free drainage water and rejects. Report botanical substrate, its moisture, retained treatment matter and structural parts separately. The common reference is 1 kg at the actual primary producer handover, with state-specific measured quantities and declared treatment/composition; no equivalence across different products is inferred. Product components are distinguished from transport packaging. Owned source production/collection, primary preparation, eligible actual treatment/holding, eligible composition assembly and final dispatch have explicit responsibility, bypass, interface, loss, stock and attribution rules. Management is conditional on actual owned production.
