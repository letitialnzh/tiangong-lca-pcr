# CPC 3.0 03249 mapping decision

## Source leaf and target

- Official label: Other parts of plants, without flowers or flower buds, and grasses, mosses and lichens, suitable for ornamental purposes.
- Source: [UNSD CPC 3.0 explanatory notes, printed p40](https://unstats.un.org/UNSDWebsite/statcom/session_56/documents/BG-3o-Explanatory_Notes_of_the_Central_Product_Classification_Version3-E.pdf).
- Canonical target: `pcr.agriculture-forestry-and-fishery-products.forestry-and-logging-products.fresh-forest-ornamental-foliage`.
- Accepted relation: `narrower`.

## Semantic boundary

Included: fresh flowerless ornamental leafy sprays, leaves, fronds and boughs from an evidenced untended forest source, with species/part, grade, freshness/moisture and actual primary collector handover declared. Forest floral-plant evidence is [USDA SRS-GTR-232](https://research.fs.usda.gov/treesearch/download/56484.pdf), chapters2 and7.

Excluded: cultivated foliage, rooted living plants, flowers/buds, whole Christmas trees, mosses/lichens and grass-only products, wreaths/bouquets/arrangements, intentionally dried/dyed/chemically preserved material, downstream distribution and use.

Route: actual forest gathering and environmental removal; actual primary trimming if performed; grade/destination acceptance; actual fresh protection, bounded preservation intervention and primary collector dispatch. Passive waiting is not a mandatory additional process or exchange. Internal process transfers do not invent sales gates or product identities. One real final output row `foliage_handover` is normalized to 1 kg net accepted fresh plant mass, excluding actual packaging tare, free added water and rejects.

This leaf also includes other ornamental parts and compositions, so this target cannot be exact. A positive narrower mapping will not claim complete methodology coverage of every class member. The broad retained empty scaffold is preserved byte-for-byte; this new canonical identity is semantic, not a classification-derived duplicate.
