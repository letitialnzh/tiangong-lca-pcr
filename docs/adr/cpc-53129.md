## Source leaf and semantic boundary

- Source leaf: `53129` — Other non-residential buildings, in the checked-in CPC 3.0 normalized leaves.
- Included product: New non-residential building in a declared eligible use with attributable fixed building systems.
- Exclusions: Industrial or commercial building; specialized non-building plant; outdoor recreation; movable fit-out; routine operation; detached equipment sale.
- Production route: Site removal; foundation and structural forming; enclosure and finishing; fixed-system integration and commissioning through signed handover.
- Product handover gate: Completed and accepted building asset, not an occupied service.
- Reference product and measure: Constructed and accepted use-qualified non-residential building. 1 m2 accepted gross floor area (GFA); report total accepted GFA.

These boundaries identify the same constructed asset named by the CPC leaf, not a sold
construction service, a manufactured component, or a later operating service. The
listed exclusions delimit adjacent CPC categories and separately measured assets.
