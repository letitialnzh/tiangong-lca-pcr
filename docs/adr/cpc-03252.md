# CPC 03252 — broad category, not the former narrow subset

## Driving decision

The user explicitly selected full methodology expansion rather than an identity-only rename. The former unmerged raw-wild-rattan-canes candidate is replaced by authored methodology at the retained broad identity `pcr.agriculture-forestry-and-fishery-products.forestry-and-logging-products.vegetable-materials-of-a-kind-used-primarily-for-plaiting-or-as-stuffing-or-padding-oth-03b445e9`. There is no published release or immutable history to rewrite. Prior narrower-scope validation/search conclusions are historical and do not certify this expanded method.

## Semantic coverage

This PCR covers vegetable materials of a kind primarily used for plaiting or as stuffing/padding, and other vegetable products within HS 2022 headings 1401 and 1404.90, excluding cotton linters. It is not restricted to fresh wild rattan. Covered families include bamboo, rattan, reeds/rushes, osier, raffia, eligible prepared cereal straw and bark plaiting materials, stuffing/padding material, broom/brush feedstock, and other vegetable products that demonstrably belong to 1404.90. Wild, managed, crop co-product and purchased-feed origins are possible and require distinct burden records.

Exclude cotton linters, textile fibres and textile goods classified elsewhere, chipwood, wood wool and prepared broom/brush knots or tufts assigned elsewhere, other wood goods outside this class, pulp/extracts and formulated chemicals classified elsewhere, and finished baskets, mats, furniture, brooms or brushes classified as articles rather than their feedstock. Primary preparation, splitting or treatment is included only while the resulting traded material remains in 1401 or 1404.90. A plant name or intended use alone cannot establish classification.

- `plaiting_material` — Cane, bamboo and other plaiting materials: Declare species, plant part, dimensions, skin/peel/split state, actual use and grade. Record cutting, sheath removal, scraping, splitting and drying separately when performed; removing a loose sheath is not epidermis scraping. A raw route does not inherit curing or bleaching.
- `stuffing_padding` — Stuffing and padding vegetable material: Identify plant origin, fibre/part state, cleaning/opening and compaction where performed. Distinguish accepted filler from seed, dust and foreign matter. Verify chapter exclusions; textile fibre is not included merely because it can fill a cushion.
- `broom_brush_material` — Broom and brush feedstock: Record species/part, length/diameter, cleaning/combing, drying and any actual bundling. End at feedstock dispatch, not the manufacture of a finished broom or brush.
- `other_vegetable_product` — Other products demonstrated to belong to 1404.90: Record product identity, specific HS eligibility, intended use, composition, source, state and actual separation/preparation. Do not arbitrarily exclude a valid residual-class product, or include every vegetable substance. A classification uncertainty must be resolved before a dataset claims this PCR is applicable.

## Method and reference

Accepted net mass of the specified traded material in its actual fresh, air-dry, dry or treated state. Include only ingredients retained in that accepted material; exclude transport packaging, free water, loose soil and rejects. Measure dry plant substrate and retained water/treatment matter separately where conversions or losses depend on them. The common reference is 1 kg at the actual primary producer handover, with state-specific measured quantities and declared treatment/composition; no equivalence across different products is inferred. Product components are distinguished from transport packaging. Owned source production/collection, primary preparation, eligible actual treatment/holding and final dispatch have explicit responsibility, bypass, interface, loss, stock and attribution rules. Management is conditional on actual owned production.
