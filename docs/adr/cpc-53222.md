# CPC 3.0 53222: Tunnels

## Decision

Accept an **exact** classification edge from CPC 3.0 `53222` (Tunnels) to
`pcr.constructions-and-construction-services.constructions.tunnels`. This record documents the mapping acceptance already recorded at
`2026-09-28T05:09:36Z` by `codex-main-agent`; it does not backdate a new review or change
the target PCR's lifecycle state.

## Source leaf and semantic boundary

- Source leaf: `53222` — Tunnels, in the checked-in CPC 3.0 normalized leaves.
- Included product: Completed road, highway, railway and underground-rail-traffic tunnel civil assets.
- Exclusions: Ordinary underpasses, stand-alone rail lines, mining tunnels and transport operation.
- Production route: Excavate and support ground; transfer accepted excavation to permanent lining and systems integration; test and hand over.
- Product handover gate: Installed and commissioned civil asset at a named site.
- Reference product and measure: Site-accepted transport tunnel. One accepted centreline-km; disclose bore-km separately.

These boundaries identify the same constructed asset named by the CPC leaf, not a sold
construction service, a manufactured component, or a later operating service. The
listed exclusions delimit adjacent CPC categories and separately measured assets.
