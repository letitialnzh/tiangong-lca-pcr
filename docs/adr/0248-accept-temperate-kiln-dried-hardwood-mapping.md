# Temperate kiln-dried planed non-coniferous sawnwood

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `31102` to `pcr.other-transportable-goods-except-metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.products-of-wood-cork-straw-and-plaiting-materials.wood-sawn-or-chipped-lengthwise-sliced-or-peeled-of-a-thickness-exceeding-6-mm-of-non-c-2b701f10`. The method supports untreated temperate non-coniferous longitudinally sawn wood, indirectly steam kiln-dried, straight-planed and dispatched with finished thickness exceeding 6 mm. Tropical species, green or air-dried final products, rough unplaned final products, sliced/peeled forms, non-steam kilns and treated or engineered wood remain outside established applicability. Botanical identity and growing ecozone determine temperate applicability; the trade term hardwood does not establish it.

The matching unpublished retained scaffold is promoted in place, with no duplicate identity. The final reference is one kg of accepted net wet kiln-dried planed product excluding packaging. Drying, planing and packing form one foreground unit with distinct stage meters and physical handoffs, avoiding duplicate terminal outputs. Green and kiln-dried identities remain distinct. Moisture, actual volume/density, joint-step coproduct allocation, internal residue fuel and separate physical-versus-burden ledgers require site records.

USDA Wood Handbook supplies measurement and wood-processing principles. CORRIM's report dated 2020-04-27 covers six northeast/north-central US hardwood mills with 2017 data; it supports regional process and allocation context, not tropical defaults or universal yields, moisture, energy and emissions. EPA's sawmill PTE memorandum excludes boilers and is not actual emission evidence. AP-42 section 1.6 separately supports conditional boiler screening. No numerical survey parameter is adopted as a universal factory quantity.
