# CPC 3.0 53112: Multi-dwelling residential buildings

## Decision

Accept an **exact** classification edge from CPC 3.0 `53112` (Multi-dwelling residential buildings) to
`pcr.constructions-and-construction-services.constructions.multi-dwelling-residential-buildings`. This record documents the mapping acceptance already recorded at
`2026-09-23T08:28:48Z` by `codex-main-agent`; it does not backdate a new review or change
the target PCR's lifecycle state.

## Source leaf and semantic boundary

- Source leaf: `53112` — Multi-dwelling residential buildings, in the checked-in CPC 3.0 normalized leaves.
- Included product: Buildings with three or more dwellings; community residences for older persons, students, workers or other social groups
- Exclusions: One- or two-dwelling buildings; non-residential buildings; separately sold construction services; standalone prefabricated components; separate civil infrastructure
- Production route: Site and foundation work, structural and envelope assembly, fixed-service integration, surface finish, testing and handover; prefabricated alternatives require explicit component and installation records
- Product handover gate: Completed construction at the declared site, ready for the declared residential use at handover
- Reference product and measure: Completed multi-dwelling residential building at the declared site One building; report gross floor area in m2 as an intensity denominator

These boundaries identify the same constructed asset named by the CPC leaf, not a sold
construction service, a manufactured component, or a later operating service. The
listed exclusions delimit adjacent CPC categories and separately measured assets.

## Evidence and readiness limit

The canonical manifest reciprocally names CPC 3.0 `53112` with an `exact` relation.
The English and Chinese PCR files and structured projection are present; the current
lifecycle pair is `candidate / authored_methodology` and Chinese translation is `aligned`.
The acceptance concerns classification identity only. Process-card, Range, Flow Set,
module-checklist and platform-flow verification remain governed by the PCR review and
validation contracts; this ADR does not certify publication readiness.

Reference-flow gap recorded by the PCR: The same-title platform candidate is an at-plant manufactured Mass flow, incompatible with one completed building at site handover.
No incompatible candidate UUID is bound merely to complete this mapping.
