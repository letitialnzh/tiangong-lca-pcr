# CPC 3.0 53251: Local pipelines

## Decision

Accept an **exact** classification edge from CPC 3.0 `53251` (Local pipelines) to
`pcr.constructions-and-construction-services.constructions.local-pipelines`. This record documents the mapping acceptance already recorded at
`2026-09-29T03:50:44Z` by `codex-main-agent`; it does not backdate a new review or change
the target PCR's lifecycle state.

## Source leaf and semantic boundary

- Source leaf: `53251` — Local pipelines, in the checked-in CPC 3.0 normalized leaves.
- Included product: Accepted local gas, potable-water, sewer, hot-water and steam pipeline segments with integral fittings, valves and chambers.
- Exclusions: Long-distance pipelines, aqueducts, treatment plants, separately contracted stations and distribution service.
- Production route: Survey and prepare corridor; excavate or install trenchlessly; join pipe and fittings; lay, protect and restore; test, condition as needed and sign handover.
- Product handover gate: Installed, tested and accepted infrastructure asset.
- Reference product and measure: A completed local pipeline asset within the signed contract boundary. One accepted contract; disclose accepted pipe length in m and installed mass in kg.

These boundaries identify the same constructed asset named by the CPC leaf, not a sold
construction service, a manufactured component, or a later operating service. The
listed exclusions delimit adjacent CPC categories and separately measured assets.
