# CPC 3.0 03111 mapping decision

## Source leaf and target

- Official label: “Roundwood of coniferous wood, sawlogs and veneer logs”; [UN CPC 3.0 subclass 03111](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Structure/Detail/EN/2100/03111).
- Canonical id: `pcr.agriculture-forestry-and-fishery-products.forestry-and-logging-products.roundwood-of-coniferous-wood-sawlogs-and-veneer-logs`.
- Accepted relation: `exact`.

## Semantic boundary and route

The leaf and proposed PCR both cover raw coniferous roundwood selected for lengthwise sawing or veneer peeling/slicing, including special saw/veneer bolts where relevant. The PCR reference is a graded log at loading-ready roadside landing, measured in cubic metres solid underbark. The [FAO forest-product definitions](https://www.fao.org/forestry-fao/7800-0944787ecbf6036088182f841ab15fe42.pdf) distinguish these logs from pulpwood, other industrial roundwood, wood fuel, sawnwood and veneer sheets. Those adjacent products may be separately reported co-products or downstream outputs, not reference volume.

The route is attributable managed-stand activity, felling/removal, ground or cable extraction, route-positioned delimbing/bucking, grading and roadside handover. Post-landing road haulage and mill conversion are excluded. [FAO wood-harvesting guidance](https://www.fao.org/sustainable-forest-management-toolbox/modules/wood-harvesting/2/en?tabInx=0) supports the distinct harvest, extraction and landing stages.

## Review findings and remaining method work

- Main review matched 15 English, Chinese, and projected flow cards by process, direction, type and row id; all 15 cards have aligned Range metadata. Targeted lint passed, and no new card uses a Flow Set.
- The route-specific managed-stand, harvest, extraction, log preparation and grading responsibilities answer the selected module obligations. Advisory checklist uncertainty on multi-output, multi-period and rework/reject is resolved by explicit allocation, period ledger and conditional rejection rules in the PCR, not by adding duplicate nodes.
- A platform result classified to CPC 03111 is actually “Wood veneer” at plant with a mass property; its product state and gate do not match raw roadside logs. It must not be used as this PCR's reference UUID.
- Exact flow, flow-property and unit-group UUIDs remain unresolved. The reference identity must be verified before active lifecycle promotion; all final TIDAS exchanges need concrete identity confirmation.
- Provisional `reasoned_estimate` Range screens are broad authoring QA aids, not publication-critical allowed values. Route-specific evidence is required before treating them as defaults.

## Decision

Accept the exact CPC 3.0 `03111` edge to this material candidate PCR: both cover coniferous sawlogs and veneer logs in unprocessed roundwood state, with adjacent roundwood uses excluded. This is a classification decision only. The unresolved reference UUID and provisional ranges keep the methodology at `candidate`, not `active` or published.
