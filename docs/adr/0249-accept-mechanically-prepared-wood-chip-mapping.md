# Mechanically prepared clean wood chips

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `31230` to `pcr.other-transportable-goods-except-metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.products-of-wood-cork-straw-and-plaiting-materials.wood-in-chips-or-particles`. The method covers clean untreated unconsolidated mechanically produced chips/particles, screening, natural storage and bulk dispatch. It excludes artificial foreground thermal drying, contaminated wood, bonded boards, densified fuel, flour/wool and bagged retail products. Dedicated chipping and wood-processing coproduct preparation preserve their distinct upstream gates and inherited burdens; a residue label or low value does not establish zero burden.

The matching unpublished retained scaffold is promoted in place. Its reference product is Wood Chip (`3fa2a6a7-224c-4d99-bd55-a214f0a83161`), with a verified Volume/m3 chain. The PCR declares one m3 of solid wood at dispatch moisture, excluding interparticle gaps. This declared convention is not inferred from the generic flow property. Identity verification, measured physical quantity and supplier/background quantity compatibility remain separate claims. Actual linked reference amounts and solid/bulk definitions, species/moisture/packing state and loss gates must agree or have a traceable measured conversion. Missing definitions or conversions leave compatibility pending and block completed background linkage or complete-dataset use. No secondary database coefficient or FAO average is treated as a matched conversion.

USDA Wood Handbook supports same-state moisture/density measurement. FAO UBET supports distinguishing wet/dry basis and solid/bulk volume; its generic conversion tables are not adopted. FAO residue-preparation guidance supplies qualitative chipping, screening and storage context. CORRIM northern sawmill evidence supplies a bounded upstream physical-accounting/allocation precedent; EPA's non-boiler sawmill memorandum supports particle screening, not actual-emission defaults or boiler factors. No universal yield, density, gas partition, storage loss or avoided-fuel credit is invented.

The controller reviewed the physical-versus-burden accounting, one terminal reference output, internal return ledgers, moisture/volume conversions and explicit unknown handling. All 33 inventory rows retain successful search evidence; 24 rows adopt 23 distinct verified identities, while nine keep concrete unresolved names. All 12 elementary rows have verified outdoor-air identities. Adopted English and official Chinese names, types, reference properties and unit groups were independently checked against state-100 raw records. The incoming and outgoing chip rows retain separate pending quantity/background compatibility conditions despite resolved product identity.

The record remains an unpublished `candidate/authored_methodology`, with author-aligned Chinese and pending independent scientific review. The accepted association establishes a bounded method, not full-leaf applicability, scientific approval or an empirically complete dataset. The target measurement check passes with complete coverage and zero skipped checks; it does not resolve the pending supplier evidence.
