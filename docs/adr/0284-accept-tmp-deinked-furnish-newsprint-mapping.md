The method covers the declared newsprint papermaking route using purchased unimpregnated TMP, washed/bleached deinked recovered-paper pulp and conditional kraft reinforcement, externally supplied electricity/steam and the declared aerobic wastewater treatment with freshwater release. It excludes integrated pulp production and invented on-site combustion.
