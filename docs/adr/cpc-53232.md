`pcr.constructions-and-construction-services.constructions.harbours-waterways-and-related-facilities`. This record documents the mapping acceptance already recorded at

## Source leaf and semantic boundary

- Source leaf: `53232` — Harbours, waterways and related facilities, in the checked-in CPC 3.0 normalized leaves.
- Included product: Accepted navigation channels and basins, port berths, quays, piers, jetties, docks, breakwaters, locks and integral marine facilities.
- Exclusions: Vessels, port operations, routine maintenance dredging, ordinary water-supply conduits, dams, irrigation and flood-control works, separately measured buildings.
- Production route: Conditional new-work dredging and sediment placement, marine foundation and structure installation, then inspection and acceptance.
- Product handover gate: Installed and accepted civil infrastructure at a named site; operating service is not the output.
- Reference product and measure: One declared navigation or port civil-works asset or bounded contract segment. 1 accepted asset or segment; separately report as-built channel length, dredged volume, berth length, structure dimensions and bill of quantities as applicable.

These boundaries identify the same constructed asset named by the CPC leaf, not a sold
construction service, a manufactured component, or a later operating service. The
listed exclusions delimit adjacent CPC categories and separately measured assets.
