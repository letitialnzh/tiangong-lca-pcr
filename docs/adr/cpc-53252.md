# CPC 3.0 53252: Local cables and related works

## Decision

Accept an **exact** classification edge from CPC 3.0 `53252` (Local cables and related works) to
`pcr.constructions-and-construction-services.constructions.local-cables-and-related-works`. This record documents the mapping acceptance already recorded at
`2026-09-29T03:50:44Z` by `codex-main-agent`; it does not backdate a new review or change
the target PCR's lifecycle state.

## Source leaf and semantic boundary

- Source leaf: `53252` — Local cables and related works, in the checked-in CPC 3.0 normalized leaves.
- Included product: Accepted local power or communication cable alignment and directly related local distribution stations, towers or antennas.
- Exclusions: Long-distance lines, component manufacture alone, delivered utility service, operation and repair.
- Production route: Survey; route/site preparation; overhead support or underground duct/trench work; cable laying and joining; optional station or tower integration; testing and handover.
- Product handover gate: Constructed, tested and accepted infrastructure at handover.
- Reference product and measure: Accepted local cable or directly related local distribution works in a declared delivery. 1 signed accepted package; separately disclose accepted route-km and station/tower/antenna counts and capacities.

These boundaries identify the same constructed asset named by the CPC leaf, not a sold
construction service, a manufactured component, or a later operating service. The
listed exclusions delimit adjacent CPC categories and separately measured assets.
