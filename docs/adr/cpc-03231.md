# Accepted CPC 03231 semantic decision

Official source label: Wild edible nuts. The CPC 3.0 explanatory note identifies gathered untended nuts, not intentionally grown. Scope proof: https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Structure/Detail/EN/2100/03231.

Proposed target is the retained semantic wild-edible-nuts record promoted from an empty scaffold after independent route review. This method covers raw in-shell wild nuts at actual gatherer/primary producer handover, including actual pre-gate collection, transfer, outer pod/husk removal, cleaning/grading, optional drying/storage and packaging. Outer pod/husk removal is not kernel shelling. FAO supports natural collection and distinct in-shell versus shelled markets: https://www.fao.org/4/x5326e/x5326e05.htm. Historical factors are not adopted.

Excluded: intentionally grown/cultivated nuts and their growing operations; shelled kernels, roasting/salting, oils and prepared foods; downstream processing/distribution. Broader wild leaf form equivalence is not proven. Proposed relation is therefore **narrower**, partial coverage, not exact.

Boundary necessity: wild-source proof and resource-removal versus upstream gathered-product ownership, actual pod/hard-shell mass state bridges, pre-gate spoilage/re-sort/stock attribution and raw in-shell gate measurement differ materially from cultivated nut methodology. No copied old PCR text establishes this decision.

Main reviewed bilingual/projected cards, modules, ranges, target checks, actual gates and the partial relation.
Unresolved reference/exchange identities leave candidate guidance review-required and are not publication readiness evidence.

## Main decision

Accept the individual narrower edge for the reviewed raw in-shell untended gathered primary scope, not whole-leaf
completion or shelled-kernel coverage. Main compared all thirty-four cards and their Range/quantity semantics,
verified compatible water-vapour and Mass/kg reuse, retained rejected roadside/farmed/pod near matches unresolved,
and checked all twelve selected modules manually. Actual direct emissions and collection protocols are explicit;
unknown additive use is a data gap, not an assumed zero. Target check: complete, 174 performed, zero skipped.

builder/research-sessions/cpc-03-round4/main-review.md. Candidate consistency is separate from active/publication
and final concrete exchange readiness.
