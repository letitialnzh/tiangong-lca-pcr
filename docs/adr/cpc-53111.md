`pcr.constructions-and-construction-services.constructions.one-and-two-dwelling-residential-buildings`. This record documents the mapping acceptance already recorded at

## Source leaf and semantic boundary

- Source leaf: `53111` — One- and two-dwelling residential buildings, in the checked-in CPC 3.0 normalized leaves.
- Included product: One site-completed building containing one or two dwellings, with declared attached scope and integrated services.
- Exclusions: Buildings with three or more dwellings; independent civil works; uninstalled prefab kits; separately sold construction services; occupant equipment.
- Production route: Site preparation and foundation; component delivery and structural/envelope assembly; services, finishing, commissioning and handover.
- Product handover gate: Permanent building installed and accepted at the named site.
- Reference product and measure: One completed one- or two-dwelling residential building. One building; report measured gross floor area in m2 as a parallel intensity denominator.

These boundaries identify the same constructed asset named by the CPC leaf, not a sold
construction service, a manufactured component, or a later operating service. The
listed exclusions delimit adjacent CPC categories and separately measured assets.
