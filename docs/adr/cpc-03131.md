# CPC 3.0 03131 mapping decision

## Source leaf and target

- Official CPC label: Fuel wood of coniferous wood. Normalized source agrees with the UNSD coordinate at https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Structure/Detail/EN/2100/03131.
- Target: `pcr.agriculture-forestry-and-fishery-products.forestry-and-logging-products.fuel-wood-of-coniferous-wood`.
- Accepted relation: `exact`, after independent main-agent product-boundary and route review.

## Semantic boundary

- Actual raw conifer wood for energy use, including declared short/split/raw branch, stick, root or stump forms with origin, bark, moisture and actual gate recorded. Raw form is not silently restricted to one retail grade.
- Excludes non-conifer or unidentified mixtures, industrial-purpose logs, deliberately manufactured chips/pellets, charcoal, treated/industrial recovered wood, heat/electricity and downstream consumer delivery.
- Source management or natural/deadwood/primary-residue collection, actual extraction and optional cutting/splitting/seasoning, destination sorting and producer roadside/collection-point handover. Source alternatives do not duplicate one mass contribution.
- A 1 kg net as-received reference does not impose universal density, water content, heating value or drying yield. Every actual form and origin must be supported in a concrete dataset.
- FAO harvesting guidance supports operations, not adoption of its charcoal gate or numerical tropical-hardwood examples; FAO forest-product definitions distinguish raw fuelwood and transformed industrial products.
