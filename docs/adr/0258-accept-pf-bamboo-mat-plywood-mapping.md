# Accept a bounded woven bamboo mat plywood association

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `31450` to `pcr.other-transportable-goods-except-metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.products-of-wood-cork-straw-and-plaiting-materials.plywood-veneer-panels-and-similar-laminated-wood-of-bamboo`.

The method covers purchased untreated, unglued woven bamboo-sliver mats, drying, aqueous PF resin dip and squeeze, maturation and redrying, layup, hot pressing, cooling, edging and packaging of unfaced bamboo mat plywood. It excludes strip/curtain boards, scrimber, overlays, carbonization, production boiling pretreatment and onsite combustion or wastewater treatment. External indirect steam or electricity is explicitly measured. The retained unpublished bamboo-panel scaffold matches this narrower physical method.

Its boiling/freezing sequence is a product quality test and is not transferred into the production route.
