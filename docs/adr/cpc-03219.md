# CPC 03219 — individual partial mapping decision

Classification coordinate: CPC 3.0 03219, “Lac, resins, balsams, natural gums and other resins n.e.c.”

Target PCR: `pcr.agriculture-forestry-and-fishery-products.forestry-and-logging-products.lac-resins-balsams-natural-gums-and-other-resins-n-e-c`.

Accepted relation: `narrower`. This is partial primary-product coverage, not exact whole-leaf coverage.

## Reviewed semantic scope

The PCR covers identified primary woody-plant exudate natural gums, resins, balsams, gum-resins and oleoresins,
and crude lac from insect secretion, with evidenced simple preparation only, at the actual producer dispatch gate.
Plant natural exudation/tapping and natural/inoculated lac harvesting are distinct conditional source operations.
The one final reference is 1 kg net as-received actual qualified product; primary internal receipts remain measured.

The leaf title has no detailed explanatory note at the UNSD detail page. A primary-only interpretation cannot prove
full-leaf coverage. Industrial refining/extraction, refined shellac, distilled rosin/turpentine and finished formulations
are outside this PCR methodology; this is not evidence that every such product is outside the classification leaf.
Rubber-like CPC 03211 gums, natural rubber, synthetic resins and non-exudate extracted gums are not substitutes.
