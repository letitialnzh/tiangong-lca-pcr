# Accept a bounded single sash spruce window association

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `31621` to `pcr.other-transportable-goods-except-metal-products-machinery-and-equipment.products-of-wood-cork-straw-and-plaiting-materials.windows-french-windows-and-their-frames-made-out-of-wood`.

The method covers complete manually operated single-sash timber windows made from purchased kiln-dried laminated spruce blanks and purchased triple-argon insulating glazing, with electric machining, actual coating, hardware/seal assembly and packaging. It excludes frame-only products and French windows. The previously authored metal-window method has an incompatible material boundary and is not duplicated. Only a narrower 31621 association is accepted.

Bau EPD's original timber-window pages 4–6 and 10–11 support complete windows, separate components and prefabrication-dependent factory stages. Four manufacturers' variants are not merged into a fixed formula; sample quantities, service life and negligible-share cutoffs are not transferred. USDA moisture guidance supports wet/oven-dry measurements. Purchased blank/glazing burdens remain upstream instead of being omitted.

Exterior Window reference 7a7a325e-e8b3-4497-bf32-fca70491694a is a factory-produced Product flow with a verified Mass/kg reference. Its PVC/cedar/vinyl examples describe exterior trim and do not impose a whole-window material exclusion. The PCR's actual wood/glazing/configuration and net mass qualifiers constrain this inclusive identity. Insulating-glass input 12053592-e6c4-4c56-ad15-a36a267c500a has Area/m2 and an aluminium spacer; actual triple glazing, spacer, area and upstream boundary must match. Its m2/kg numerator uses delivered glazing area, not opening area or an invented kg/m2 factor.

All 38 rows were searched; 13 rows use 12 distinct verified UUIDs, including all five elementary rows; 25 specific identities remain unresolved. Complete supplier composition, per-layer usage, retained film, returns/losses and air/waste fate are collected through atomic recipe rows. Unknown actual ingredients block composition closure and require their own rows; they do not justify an invented universal coating or adhesive recipe. Constituent and formulated-product backgrounds cannot be double counted. Cohort net complete-window mass excludes packaging and remains consistent across languages.

Independent review checked raw state-100 identities, exact official English/Chinese names, flow types, media, chemical origins and the reference property-to-unit-group chain, as well as bilingual measurement and original source applicability. Identity verification remains distinct from actual quantity and supplier/background compatibility. The unpublished method remains `candidate/authored_methodology`, author-aligned in Chinese and pending independent scientific review. This association does not establish full classification coverage, publication or empirical dataset completeness.
