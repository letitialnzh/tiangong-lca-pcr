# CPC 3.0 53261: Mining constructions

## Decision

Accept an **exact** classification edge from CPC 3.0 `53261` (Mining constructions) to
`pcr.constructions-and-construction-services.constructions.mining-constructions`. This record documents the mapping acceptance already recorded at
`2026-09-29T04:17:39Z` by `codex-main-agent`; it does not backdate a new review or change
the target PCR's lifecycle state.

## Source leaf and semantic boundary

- Source leaf: `53261` — Mining constructions, in the checked-in CPC 3.0 normalized leaves.
- Included product: Site-accepted mine-specific shafts, drifts, winding towers, loading/discharge stations and related non-building civil facilities.
- Exclusions: Mineral extraction and processing; standalone buildings; non-mine tunnels; separately sold equipment; mine operation and future maintenance.
- Production route: Survey and prepare ground; remove ground for shaft/drift routes; form permanent support or foundation; integrate structural, hoisting, loading and ventilation interfaces as contracted; test and hand over. Surface-only towers/stations omit underground excavation.
- Product handover gate: Installed, tested and accepted facility at a named mine site.
- Reference product and measure: One functional mine-specific civil facility accepted on site. One accepted facility; disclose excavated volume, supported length and structural quantities separately.

These boundaries identify the same constructed asset named by the CPC leaf, not a sold
construction service, a manufactured component, or a later operating service. The
listed exclusions delimit adjacent CPC categories and separately measured assets.
