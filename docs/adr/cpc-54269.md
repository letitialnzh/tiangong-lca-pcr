## Reviewed semantic target

- Canonical PCR: `pcr.existing-ferrous-foundry-coreless-induction-melting-plant-integrated-reconstruction-and-commissioning-services`.
- Included product: Integrated retained-plant civil and process-interface reconstruction services with actual lining and hot-trial acceptance
- Excluded adjacent products/routes: Exclude a whole new foundry, generic industrial building, nonferrous-only or blast/cupola/EAF-only plant, separately supplied furnace machinery, isolated erection/relining/routine maintenance, cold-only installation, and ordinary commercial melting or casting after handover. Capacity, vessel count, lining chemistry, coolant, heat duration, test temperature, energy/yield, emissions and asset life are project-specific; supplier examples are no defaults. Acceptance is neither a universal operating procedure nor an automatic safety, emissions-compliance or lifetime-performance certificate. unsd-cpc3-industrial-plants-2025; hse-foundry-furnace-fd2-2022.
- Actual route: Retained survey and isolation; actual removal/civil modification; integrated installation; progressive cold checks; required lining commissioning; supervised hot trials; defects closed and signed handover
- Starting condition: Surveyed retained foundry building, equipment, utilities, existing lining and metal heel with approved reconstruction package and responsibility limits
- Handover/market state: Completed accepted site-specific integrated construction service on an existing plant, not a tonne of metal or a purchased furnace
- Acceptance quality: Approved project-specific civil and integrated cold/hot acceptance dossier, closed defects and signed owner/contractor handover
- Reference convention: 1 complete accepted intervention; From retained survey through actual removal/civil, integration, first-lining and cold/hot trial, correction and handover; link all reporting periods without a standard duration.
