## Source leaf and semantic boundary

- Source leaf: `53270` — Outdoor sport and recreation facilities, in the checked-in CPC 3.0 normalized leaves.
- Included product: Accepted outdoor sport or recreation facility within one declared construction contract.
- Exclusions: Indoor sport installation; independently accepted building, road or utility; component sale; routine operation and maintenance.
- Production route: Survey/removal; earthwork and base forming; route-specific component integration; optional distinct surface or landscape finish; inspection, remediation and signed handover.
- Product handover gate: Constructed and accepted outdoor asset, not attendance or playing hours.
- Reference product and measure: Constructed outdoor sport or recreation facility of declared family and scope. One signed, accepted facility; report developed area in m2 and route-specific capacity separately.

These boundaries identify the same constructed asset named by the CPC leaf, not a sold
construction service, a manufactured component, or a later operating service. The
listed exclusions delimit adjacent CPC categories and separately measured assets.
