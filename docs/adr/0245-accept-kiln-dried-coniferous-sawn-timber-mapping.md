# Kiln-dried coniferous sawn timber

Accept a `narrower` association from CPC 3.0 `31101` to the existing semantic identity for coniferous timber exceeding 6 mm. The directly supported methodology covers unimpregnated kiln-dried longitudinally sawn/chipped coniferous timber, rough or straight-planed, at the mill gate. The accepted edge does not establish applicability to every product or route within the classification leaf. Green and air-dried dispatch, sliced/peeled stock and other excluded or unreviewed routes require a separate applicability decision and complete reference definition before use.

The retained empty scaffold is promoted in place; no duplicate identity or classification-code directory is created. Its distinctive methodology concerns moisture and density basis, drying and finishing, stage-specific coproduct allocation, internal residue fuel, disjoint particulate fractions, individual volatile compounds and separate biogenic/fossil carbon. It requires site measurements and a compatible upstream boundary rather than importing a regional average as a universal parameter.

USDA's 2021 Wood Handbook supports moisture and measurement distinctions. The 2025 CORRIM Northern softwood lumber report supports process and allocation context for its stated regional scope. EPA AP-42 section 1.6 supports wood-boiler screening; EPA's 2014 sawmill memorandum applies to non-boiler potential-to-emit screening and is not an actual-emission default. The canonical PCR records the exact source URLs and conditions.
