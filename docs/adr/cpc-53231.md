# CPC 3.0 53231: Aqueducts and other water supply conduits, except pipelines

## Decision

Accept an **exact** classification edge from CPC 3.0 `53231` (Aqueducts and other water supply conduits, except pipelines) to
`pcr.constructions-and-construction-services.constructions.aqueducts-and-other-water-supply-conduits-except-pipelines`. This record documents the mapping acceptance already recorded at
`2026-09-29T02:02:21Z` by `codex-main-agent`; it does not backdate a new review or change
the target PCR's lifecycle state.

## Source leaf and semantic boundary

- Source leaf: `53231` — Aqueducts and other water supply conduits, except pipelines, in the checked-in CPC 3.0 normalized leaves.
- Included product: Completed non-pipeline channels, canals and aqueduct structures for water supply.
- Exclusions: Pipelines, irrigation/flood-control systems, dams, navigation structures, pumping/treatment, operation and delivered water.
- Production route: Survey and prepare corridor; excavate and form support; build or line hydraulic section; join structure, lining and joints; test and hand over.
- Product handover gate: Installed, tested and accepted fixed infrastructure at a named site.
- Reference product and measure: Site-accepted non-pipeline water-supply conduit segment. One route-kilometre along accepted centreline, measured once.

These boundaries identify the same constructed asset named by the CPC leaf, not a sold
construction service, a manufactured component, or a later operating service. The
listed exclusions delimit adjacent CPC categories and separately measured assets.

## Evidence and readiness limit

The canonical manifest reciprocally names CPC 3.0 `53231` with an `exact` relation.
The English and Chinese PCR files and structured projection are present; the current
lifecycle pair is `candidate / authored_methodology` and Chinese translation is `aligned`.
The acceptance concerns classification identity only. Process-card, Range, Flow Set,
module-checklist and platform-flow verification remain governed by the PCR review and
validation contracts; this ADR does not certify publication readiness.

Reference-flow gap recorded by the PCR: Platform candidate search returned a pumped-storage waterworks flow and a groundwater well under this classification; neither matches a general site-accepted non-pipeline water-supply conduit. Reference flow, property and unit-group UUIDs remain blank pending exact detail-confirmed identity.
No incompatible candidate UUID is bound merely to complete this mapping.
