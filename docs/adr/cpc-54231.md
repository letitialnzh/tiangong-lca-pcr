## Reviewed semantic target

- Canonical PCR: `pcr.existing-water-supply-aqueduct-hand-applied-cementitious-concrete-patch-repair-services`.
- Included product: Finally accepted hand-applied one-part cementitious local spall repair service on retained non-pipeline water-supply concrete aqueduct/channel
- Excluded adjacent products/routes: New conduit; irrigation/flood/navigation works; pipelines; sprayed full lining; crack injection; joint sealant; new/replaced reinforcement or major structural strengthening
- Actual route: Qualify/isolate/access → local removal and cleaning → conditional approved rebar protection/primer → measured powder/water batches → hand scratch coat, compaction and profile → protected curing → inspection/rework and signed hygienic handover
- Starting condition: Surveyed existing water-supply concrete aqueduct with local spalling, retained sound substrate and potentially exposed retained reinforcement; owner-authorised repair scope and actual operational/isolation state
- Handover/market state: Completed installed repair service at owner acceptance; neither dry mortar sale nor fresh mortar supply
- Acceptance quality: Actual approved water-contact mortar/system; qualified retained sound substrate/rebar; completed required cure; accepted geometry/bond and owner hygienic gate
- Reference convention: 1 m3 of unique finally accepted installed geometric patch volume; One bounded job including all attempts, preparation, cure, tests and defect closure until signed handover; link stock carry and shared-asset manufacture, operation/maintenance/replacement and termination/end-of-life phases across all relevant periods; no assumed service life.
